Research Interests

I work to understand how human learn and reason. I'm currently investigating how humans extract useful information from a complex environment, and how they form abstract mental representations in an effective way.

I try to answer these questions through studying the human innate cognitive toolkits, including game-playing, eye-moving and use of language, primarily by conducting behavioral experiments and building computational models including normative models like RL and Bayesian models and expectedly also neural networks.

I'm also interested in research in developmental cognition, I hope to investigate how the intricate human minds are built up, by interacting with children in their early stage of development. I'm looking forward to collaborate with researchers experienced in techniques like real-life video analysis, artificial neural networks and neuroimaging as well.

  • Human learning, memory, attention, and reasoning.
  • Computational cognitive modeling and theory-driven approaches to cognition.
  • Human-AI interaction, especially interpretability, trust, and collaborative intelligence.
  • Interdisciplinary questions connecting cognitive science, psychology, neuroscience, and AI.
